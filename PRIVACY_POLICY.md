# Privacy Policy

**Effective date:** September 8, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Image Compressor & Converter Suite handles information when you use the application.

## 1. Overview

Image Compressor & Converter Suite is a browser-based image processing tool. The application is designed to process images locally on your device using browser APIs and HTML Canvas.

## 2. Images and Personal Data

- Images selected in the application are processed locally in your browser.
- Images are not uploaded to or stored on an application server.
- The application does not require an account, name, email address, or other personal information to process images.
- Processed images are saved only when you choose to download them through your browser.
- When batch processing is used, selected files, queue status, and generated results remain in browser memory for the current page session. Individual downloads and ZIP creation also happen locally.

You remain responsible for ensuring that you have the right to process any image you select.

## 3. Cookies and Local Storage

The current application does not intentionally use cookies, analytics trackers, advertising trackers, or persistent local storage to collect user information.

Your browser may temporarily manage object URLs, decoded images, generated Blobs, and cached resources while the application is running. Replaced previews and reset operations release managed object URLs; remaining temporary resources are released when the page is closed.

## 4. Third-Party Services

The application does not intentionally send uploaded images to third-party processing services. It does not currently depend on external image-processing APIs, ZIP services, CDNs, or external analytics services. Batch ZIP files are assembled by local JavaScript in the browser.

If the application is later hosted through a third-party platform, that platform may collect technical information according to its own privacy policy.

## 5. Downloads

When you select **Download Image**, the processed file is transferred to your browser's normal download location. The application does not control the browser's download folder or retain a copy of the downloaded file.

## 6. Security

Local processing helps reduce the risk of image content being transmitted over a network. However, no software or device can be guaranteed to be completely secure. Keep your browser and operating system updated and use the application only on devices you trust.

## 7. Children's Privacy

The application does not knowingly collect personal information from children or any other users. Because the application does not require registration or personal data collection, no age-based account is provided.

## 8. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when the application's data handling or functionality changes. The effective date at the top of this document will be updated when revisions are made.

## 9. Contact

For privacy questions, contact the repository owner or maintainer through the contact method provided with the project repository.

This document describes the current behavior of the standalone application and is provided for general informational purposes. It is not legal advice. If the application is deployed as part of a larger website or service, this policy should be reviewed and updated to reflect that deployment environment.
